## Design implications

- **Discretion is a feature.** People arrive at this site about erections, kink, porn use,
  bereavement. The page should read as unembarrassed and matter-of-fact, never coy and never
  clinical-cold. Plain words, no euphemism, no stock-photo sadness.
- **Two audiences, one page.** Clients, and therapists looking for a supervisor. Supervision
  needs its own clear section, not a footnote.
- **Inclusive by default** — LGBTQ+, poly and throuple dynamics, 11+. He already writes this
  way; the design should not straighten it out.
- **Phone and email everywhere.** `tel:` and `mailto:` links, plus a WhatsApp option, repeated
  down the scroll. No form as the primary path.
- **Mobile first.** Therapy sites are read on phones, often privately.
