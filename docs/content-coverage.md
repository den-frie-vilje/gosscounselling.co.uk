# Coverage of the old site — what to keep, what to drop

Checked the three directions against [content-scrape.md](content-scrape.md), claim by claim.

## Everything substantive is carried

All 27 substantive claims from the old site appear in all three directions: the title he uses, the
location, in-person and online, qualifying in 2015, the integrative approach, all four services,
the full seven-item symptom list, porn/fetish/kink, the supervision framing in his own words, every
fee, the low-income offer, all ten qualifications, all three membership bodies, both contact
routes, and all five FAQs.

The only claim that varies in prominence is the **11+ age range**. Quiet Practice and Clear Water
carry it as a label above the individual counselling block; Full Colour states it in the hero and
again in the "won't have to explain" list. That is a register difference rather than a gap, but if
parents of teenagers are a group he wants, the Full Colour treatment is the one that reaches them.

## Deliberately not carried

| Dropped | Why |
| --- | --- |
| The blog | Two real posts and one placeholder reading "Blogs coming soon!". A blog that stalls reads worse than no blog. Recommend launching without `/blog` and adding it if he wants to write. |
| `sample-page`, `test` | WordPress leftovers, both empty. |
| HealthHosts login and branding assets | Belong to the host he is leaving. |
| The AdobeStock illustration library | 42 items uploaded by the previous designer; the licence almost certainly sits with them, not with him. All three directions are built to work without them. |
| "Learn more" / "Read more" teaser stubs | The old home page was a set of teasers pointing at thin pages. The scroll now carries the content itself, and each service links to a future detail page instead. |

## Worth keeping but currently thin

Three things exist on the old site that deserve better than they had, and that I would raise with
him rather than decide for him:

1. **"Therapy: talking about the cost"** — his own post, and the best writing on the old site. It
   answers a question people actually ask and it earns links. It is currently compressed into the
   "why is therapy so expensive" FAQ. If a blog launches, this is the first post; if not, it could
   become a short section on the fees page.
2. **"Celebrating LGBT History Month"** — a signal of who he is at ease with, currently reduced to
   a single line on the old site. In the Full Colour direction that signal is carried by the copy
   instead. Worth asking whether he wants it as a post or as a standing statement.
3. **His prison, Samaritans and youth-mentoring background** — one clause in his about text, and
   the most distinctive thing on the page for anyone weighing up whether he has heard difficult
   things before. All three directions keep it; it could carry more weight on `/about`.

## Two things the old site never said, which he may want to

Flagged because their absence is conspicuous for the audiences arriving, not because I have
invented an answer:

- **What confidentiality covers.** Nothing on the old site addresses it, and it is the second
  question most people have after cost. It needs his words, not mine.
- **Whether he has a waiting list, and how quickly he answers.** People ringing about a sexual
  difficulty or a bereavement are often deciding between three therapists on availability alone.

## Recommendation

Launch with the scroll plus `/counselling/psychosexual-therapy`, `/fees` and `/contact`, drop the
blog until he wants to write, and ask him for two paragraphs: one on confidentiality, one on
availability. Everything else on the old site is either already carried or better left behind.
