Four directions for gosscounselling.co.uk

The same practice, four different temperaments — and four different voices, since a look without a register is only half a choice. Every fee, qualification and service is identical across all four; what changes is what gets said first, and who the page is visibly speaking to.

Prototypes, not the finished site. The words are John's own, lightly edited.

What to look for

They differ on temperature, on what the page leads with, and on who it is visibly addressing — not on what it says:

  • A opens with a portrait and a sentence. Quietest; assumes the visitor already knows they want therapy.
  • B puts the phone number in a gradient band under the hero, then runs the practice as colour blocks. The colour does quiet work — it says this is somewhere you can bring anything, without a badge announcing it.
  • C answers the practical questions before they are asked, and is the strongest of the four for someone comparing therapists in three tabs.
  • D is closest in shape to the practices John pointed at. Weighted for a general audience, but psychosexual therapy and supervision are written out in full, so the two groups who need detail still get it.

Notes

All four treat sexual difficulties in plain words, keep John's own “couples, throuples or poly dynamics”, and lead with tel: and mailto: — there is no booking system anywhere.

Each service block already links to a detail page that doesn't exist yet, so the single scroll can grow into /counselling/psychosexual-therapy and its siblings without a redesign. Those links come back here for now.

Mixing is expected. A palette from one and a structure from another is a normal outcome of this stage.
